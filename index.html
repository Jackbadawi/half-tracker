Strength & safety

This plan squeezes 5 weeks into 4, so recovery matters. Keep strength sessions light the day before hard runs, and skip heavy legs before the long run.

If you feel sharp pain, stop the session. Don't run the next sessions until it's checked and you can walk normally without pain.

Paces are based on the 1:55 goal. If easy runs feel hard, slow down. Easy days should feel easy.

Plan start

Pick the Monday of Week 1. The days shown are only suggestions, spaced so hard runs aren't back to back. Do the sessions on any day that suits you, just try to keep them in order within the week. Race day is Sunday of Week 4.

Level

The program has two versions of the workouts and long runs. Beginner uses the (B) sessions and Intermediate uses the (I) ones.
